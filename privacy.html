Privacy Policy for Snap Expense

Last Updated: July 2026

Snap Expense ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the Snap Expense mobile application.

1. Data Collection & Processing

Snap Expense is designed with user privacy in mind:

  • Local Storage: Expense records, merchant details, dates, and amounts are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. We do not maintain external database servers storing your personal expense history.
  • AI Receipt Parsing: When you scan a receipt, the image is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Google's Gemini API strictly for optical character recognition (OCR) and structured text extraction. Images are processed in real-time and are not stored by us.

2. In-App Purchases & Billing

Subscription and purchase transactions are processed securely by Apple's App Store and managed via RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.

3. Third-Party Services

We only share data with third-party service providers necessary to deliver core application functionality:

  • Google Gemini API: For automated AI image parsing and receipt data extraction.
  • RevenueCat & Apple App Store: For validating subscription entitlements and managing Pro tier access.

4. Analytics & Tracking

Snap Expense does not track you across third-party apps or websites, nor do we sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your expense data resides locally on your device, deleting the Snap Expense application will permanently erase all stored local records.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at earths-unreal-7b@icloud.com.
