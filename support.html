Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Snap Expense, navigate to Settings or the Upgrade screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Are my receipts stored on external servers?

No. Your expense database and receipt history are stored locally on your device using native Apple storage for privacy and instant offline access.

How do I export my data for tax filing?

Go to the main list or reports view and tap the Export button to generate a clean CSV file compatible with Excel, QuickBooks, or your accountant.
