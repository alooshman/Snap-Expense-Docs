Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Snap Expense, navigate to Settings or the Upgrade screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Are my receipts stored on external servers?

No. Your expense records and receipt database are stored locally on your device and synced securely across your devices via your personal iCloud account. We do not maintain external database servers to store your history. When scanning a receipt or importing a document/PDF, image files are transmitted securely over an encrypted connection solely for automated real-time optical character recognition (OCR) and structured data extraction. Data is processed ephemerally, is never reviewed by human annotators, is never used to train artificial intelligence models, is never used to track users across services, and is not permanently stored or retained on remote servers.

How do I export my data for tax filing?

Go to the main list or reports view and tap the Export button. You can generate a compiled, audit-ready PDF expense report (with receipt attachments) or a clean CSV spreadsheet compatible with Excel, QuickBooks, Numbers, or your accountant.
